A sleeve squeezes the whole knee. A hinged brace stops it going sideways, and her knee was never going sideways. A gel sits on the skin. None of them move the load. This does. It is called Stryde. It sits two centimetres below the kneecap, on the tendon. It never crosses the joint. A silicone pad inside holds pressure on that one band instead of spreading it round the whole knee. The weight gets caught and moved off the worn part before it reaches the joint. The placement is the whole thing. A centimetre too high and it is a sleeve again.

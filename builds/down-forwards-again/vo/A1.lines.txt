A patient of mine. Nine years of knee pain. Bone on bone on the left, the right one following it. Here is what her scan does not show you. Two centimetres below your kneecap there is a band of tendon about as wide as your thumb. Every step you take lands on it. Seventeen times your bodyweight. Put your finger under your kneecap and press. That band is the one taking it. Coming down is worse than going up. Going up, your muscles lift you. Coming down, you are catching yourself, and the catch lands on that band.

## Hooks and why
- **HK1: "…anyone who dreads the stairs."** This is the script's own identity callout. Stairs are the pain the whole body pays off: B14, B24 and B26 all land on stairs. It opens on Carol at the foot of the stairs, bracing on the rail.
- **HK2: "…bone on bone knees."** It names a condition, which is the strongest self-selection for arthritis sufferers. It opens on Carol wincing on the sofa. It is also the shortest (3.7s), so the body starts soonest.
- **HK3: "…your knee hurts going downstairs."** Descending is the most specific trigger and the hardest moment for a painful knee. It opens on Carol coming down one step at a time, holding the rail.

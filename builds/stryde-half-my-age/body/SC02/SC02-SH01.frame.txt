For the line "Just leave it by the stairs, love. I’ll get it later.": she stands stranded at the top of her stairs at night, calling down. SCENE MASTER, Scene 2. A full-length shot from low at the foot of the stairs, three-quarter on, looking up the straight flight to the landing. Image 1 is this hall: stairs up the left-hand wall, dark banister and square spindles on the open right side, framed photographs up the wall, brass stair rods, oatmeal carpet — copied exactly. On the landing stands the woman from Image 2 — face, steel-grey bob with its heavy fringe and small slight build copied exactly, in a heather-green jumper, charcoal A-line skirt and grey slippers. Her right hand rests on the top newel post, her left hand loose at her side; she is looking down the stairs, mouth open mid-word, about a third of the frame's height. Two plain shopping bags stand on the carpet at the foot of the bottom step; the telephone table holds only the telephone. Night: hall pendant and landing lamp, warm 2800K, key from the right; the rest of the house deep blue-grey. Clothing and bags plain and unbranded. A still from a British prestige drama, ARRI Alexa, natural colour, real texture.

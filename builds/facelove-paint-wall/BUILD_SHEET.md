### Part 3 — Style Lock (copied)

- **Format:** one woman presenter in her late fifties explains a skin problem with a physical analogy built full-size in a clean set, walking through it and pointing at it, then reveals the product as the one that works.
- **Visual grammar:** bright, clean, almost shadowless white-and-cream set; the presenter full-length in the wide, then medium/medium-close for the punchlines; the analogy objects big and literal in frame behind or beside her; dry, knowing humour in her face.
- **Light:** high-key soft daylight look, white walls, gentle floor reflection, no hard shadows.
- **Wardrobe:** one simple outfit all the way through (black cap-sleeve top, cream wide-leg trousers) — ours is the avatar's oatmeal linen blazer over a white tank and cream trousers.
- **Rhythm:** long takes with jump-ins on emphasis; the gag lands, she lets it sit.
- **Tone:** confident teacher, a little wry ("Don't get excited"), never salesy until the end.

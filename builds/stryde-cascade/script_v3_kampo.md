## Flag F21 (new): a generated physician endorsing a medical device
A fictional character presented as a doctor endorses the product, while repeating "Seventeen times your bodyweight", "Thirty four percent less strain. Measured. Three years with orthopedic surgeons." FTC Endorsement Guides §255.3 (expert endorsements) and the ASA CAP Code (3.45; 12.x on health professionals) treat an implied professional endorsement as a claim that must be real. The advertiser's counsel decides before this runs. A "dramatisation" super is the usual minimum.

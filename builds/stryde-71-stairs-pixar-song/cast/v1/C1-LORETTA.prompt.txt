The one contrasting shape is her round silver crop of hair, a single circle on top of all those straight lines. One dominant shape drives the character — round, square, or triangular — and every major mass repeats it: head, torso, hands, overall silhouette. One charm marker breaks the symmetry — a cowlick, a gap tooth, one ear sitting higher, freckles on one side only.

WHAT THE AD IS
An AI Drama VSL, photoreal, like a short film. Port of the Lymphoria drama (reference above): a man whose body fails him at the one moment his wife needs him, in front of a younger man. Fully acted in dialogue, no narrator until the offer. The only voice-over is Tony’s inner thought, plus Sue’s once. British accents throughout.
 
CASTING
TONY, 64: his face looks his age (salt and pepper, strong builder’s hands, broad shoulders), but he moves like a man of 85. Do NOT age his face. SUE, 52, looks 45 to 48: active, well kept, quick. GARY, 70, fit, lifts slabs on his own. The same LAD in the hook and the payoff. The same car park in the hook and the payoff.
 
THE STORY
The car park. At the garden centre, Sue is loading the boot when a van starts reversing towards her. Tony sees it from ten feet away and shouts, drops the potted plant he’s holding, and his knee gives out on the first step. A young lad from the garden centre sprints past him and pulls Sue clear. Then he asks her: “Is your dad alright?” Sue doesn’t correct him.
The car. Sue is shaken. “If that lad hadn’t been there, Tony…” She doesn’t finish. “You didn’t make it one step.” Tony promises he’ll sort it.
Rock bottom. Thirty years laying patios, and now he comes down his own stairs sideways. The GP says it’s wear and tear, “very normal”. A drawer full of braces that never worked.
The mentor. At the builders’ yard, Gary, 70, is lifting slabs on his own. Two years ago he was worse than Tony. He makes Tony press one spot under the kneecap: that’s where it hurts. It isn’t worn-out knees, it’s that one spot taking seventeen times your bodyweight. “You’re not old. You’re overloaded.” What fixed it: Stryde, a strap that sits on that spot.
The doubt. Sue finds the strap: “You’ve said that before.” Tony: “Then don’t watch.”
The change. He comes down the stairs forwards for the first time in two years, and doesn’t tell her. A labourer and a neighbour notice. Sue finds him on his knees in the garden, laying their new patio.
The payoff. Same car park, weeks later. A car pulls out fast towards Sue, and this time Tony gets to her in three strides. The same lad saw it: “You alright, sir?” Tony: “She’s fine. Thanks, love.” He swings a 25 kilo bag of compost into the boot. In the car, she kisses him.
The offer. A narrator closes over the strap, the stairs, the patio and the pack: it’s not your age, it’s one overloaded spot. Buy one get one free, 60 days to send it back.
